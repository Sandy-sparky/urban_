RAJKOT · 2020

Night-time radiance & population

VIIRS monthly average radiance is summarised into an annual surface. The highest quartile is computed across pixels within the selected municipal boundary. WorldPop population is then summed across the cells that intersect that mask.

  1. Inspect monthly changes in mean radiance.
  2. Locate bright zones in the annual map.
  3. Compare city population with population in upper-quartile radiance pixels.
This measures the estimated population residing in areas with relatively high satellite-observed radiance. It does not measure individual light exposure, wealth, safety, or electricity access.

04 / Method

Transparent by design.

The method follows the assignment’s requirements: define the study boundary, filter the right time period, produce maps and dynamic output, report numeric summaries, and discuss limitations.

STEP 01

Define the city

Use a city/ULB boundary GeoJSON, check the feature and source, and keep the same geometry across all statistics.

STEP 02

Filter observations

Filter VIIRS to Jan–Dec 2020 and the boundary. Use radiance and cloud-free coverage bands with quality caveats.

STEP 03

Quantify the upper quartile

Calculate the city-specific 75th percentile of annual mean radiance and mask pixels at or above that threshold.

STEP 04

Summarise population

Sum WorldPop population for the city and for the masked region; report the exposed-population share and limitations.
